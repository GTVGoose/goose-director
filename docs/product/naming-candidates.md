# Product naming — vetted candidates (2026-07-16)

**Status:** name is OPEN. "Nexus" was a working title; "Healix" was floated in today's meeting.
Every candidate below was checked against live web results on 2026-07-16 (collision = an existing
software/AI product or funded company using the name).

## What the sweep proved

Nearly every English word meaning "a council/deliberation" already has an AI company on it. The
deliberation concept is being productized *right now* (see competitive-landscape section of the
MVP plan) — which means the name war for council-words is already lost. The opportunity is a name
from a lane nobody in AI is using.

## Survivor (verified clean in this space)

**Asterism** — an *asterism* is a recognizable pattern of stars that isn't an official
constellation, and ⁂ is the asterism typographic mark: literally three stars grouped as one glyph.
Many lights, one figure — the product concept in a single word, with the logo built into Unicode.
Collisions found: only offshore IT-services/staffing firms (Asterism InfoSoft, Asterism IT
Solutions) — no consumer product, no AI product, no Mac software. Risks: needs a beat of
explanation; slightly bookish.

## The recommended direction: fly the goose flag

The studio's identity is *Goose*. Birds-in-formation words are (a) a perfect metaphor for many
models moving as one, (b) essentially untouched in AI, and (c) turn the founder story into the
brand instead of hiding it. Candidates, strongest first — **each needs one quick web/trademark
check before use** (same 5-minute sweep as above):

1. **Second Skein** — Goose's riff (2026-07-16), now the front-runner. Plays on *second skin*
   (an extension of yourself), *second brain* (the PKM concept — literally the Pro tier), and
   *skein* (a goose flock in flight — your second flock of minds). **Exact phrase verified clean:
   zero products, brands, or companies.** Caveats: skein is pronounced "skayn," so the skin pun
   fully lands only in text; expect "skien" misspellings. Remaining diligence: secondskein.com,
   App Store, USPTO, handles.
2. **Skein** (solo) — *demoted 2026-07-16 after deeper check:* more contested than first thought —
   Skein AI Systems (skeinai.com), Skein healthtech AI (skein.co), Skein Technologies (dev shop),
   skein.dev, KoboldAI "Skein" story model. The compound form above is what makes it ownable.
2. **Gander** — male goose + *"take a gander"* = take a look. Double meaning lands perfectly:
   "Take a gander — ask every AI at once." Known uses: defunct outdoor retailer (different world).
3. **Murmuration** — a starling flock moving as one organism. The most beautiful metaphor of the
   set and the demo GIF writes itself; long word, and murmuration.org (political data nonprofit)
   exists. Wordmark could shorten to **Murmur** (check: murmur has some audio-app uses).
4. **Gaggle** — a gaggle of geese; playful, memorable, maximally on-brand. Known risk:
   Gaggle.net is an established K-12 safety-monitoring company (different market, but a real
   trademark presence — check hardest).
5. **Aviary** — many birds, one house. The old Adobe-acquired photo app is long dead; the name
   may be revivable. Softer fit than the formation words.

Tagline that works with any of them: *"One question. The whole flock."*
